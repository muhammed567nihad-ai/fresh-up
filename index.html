Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our ingredients, bottling, freshness, and cold delivery.

Yes, absolutely! Every single bottle of our Fresh and Flavored juices is cold-pressed from authentic fresh fruit sourced directly from partner orchards across India. We never use artificial flavors, chemical preservatives, or synthetic food dyes.

Our cold-pressed fresh juices stay crisp and nutritious for 21 days refrigerated (kept under 4°C) using gentle High-Pressure Processing (HPP) which preserves live enzymes without thermal pasteurization. Our sparkling sodas have an unopened shelf life of 6 months.

We offer 4 standard sizes: Small (250ml) - pocket refresher; Regular (350ml) - daily thirst quencher; Large (500ml) - hydration hero; and Party Jug (1L) - perfect for family dinners and parties.

All orders are packed in thermal insulated eco-friendly boxes with food-grade gel ice packs. Deliveries in metro cities arrive within 2 to 4 hours icy cold, right to your doorstep.

100% yes! All our juices, botanicals, and sparkling sodas are completely vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free, and crafted in a clean nut-free facility.
