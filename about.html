Our Mission & Flavor

The Story Behind ZIP ZIP

We got tired of overly sweet synthetic sodas and dull watered-down juices. So we set out on a colorful mission: bring authentic orchard freshness together with playful fizz.

Born in Sunlit Orchards

From Tree Branch to Chilled Bottle in Under 24 Hours

ZIP ZIP was founded by beverage artisans, fruit farmers, and flavor obsessives who believed that real fruit doesn't need artificial makeup. Our cold-press facilities are located right at the edge of verified sustainable orchards across Ratnagiri, Mahabaleshwar, Nagpur, and the Himalayan foothills.

Instead of boiling fruit into lifeless concentrates, we utilize gentle cold hydraulic pressure and High-Pressure Processing (HPP) to preserve cellular vitamins, crisp fruit aromas, and vibrant natural hues.

Cold Crafted Process

How We Make the Magic Happen

🌳

1. Hand-Pick

Harvested early in the morning when fruit sugar and hydration are peak.

❄️

2. Cold-Press

Gentle hydraulic crushing with zero heat to safeguard live enzymes.

🧪

3. Botanical Blend

Infused with natural herbs, spearmint, lime zest, or fine champagne bubbles.

🧊

4. Deep Chill

Brought to a brisk 3°C instantly to lock in crisp effervescence.

⚡

5. ZIP Delivery

Dispatched in thermal coolers right to your door in 2 hours.
